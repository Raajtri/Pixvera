Many business owners in Mumbai build a following on Instagram first, and for good reason. It is free to start, visual, and easy to use from a phone. So when someone says you need a website too, it is fair to ask: why?

This article gives a balanced answer. Instagram is genuinely useful. It also has limits that matter for a business. Let us look at both.

## What Instagram does well

- **Shows your work.** Photos and short videos let people see your food, designs, results or products quickly.
- **Builds a relationship.** Stories, reels and replies make customers feel they know you.
- **Reaches people who were not searching.** The right post can introduce you to someone who did not know you existed.
- **Is easy to update.** You can post from your phone in minutes.
- **Starts conversations.** Direct messages let customers ask questions easily.

If you sell something visual, Instagram is a strong tool. We are not suggesting you stop using it.

## Where Instagram falls short for a business

### 1. You do not own the account in the way you own a website

Your Instagram account lives on a platform you do not control. Rules change, accounts can be restricted or hacked, and your followers belong to the platform's system. A website you own, on a domain registered in your name, stays yours.

### 2. Many customers start on Google, not Instagram

When someone needs a service, they often type it into Google. They look for the business name, the address, reviews and a phone number. Instagram profiles do show up in search sometimes, but a website and Google Business Profile are designed for this moment. If you want to understand that second part, read our [guide to setting up a Google Business Profile](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-business-profile-step-by-step/).

### 3. It is hard to explain detail

Instagram is great for a picture and a caption. It is less good for things customers want to read before deciding: full list of services, process, pricing guidance, policies, FAQs and location details. A website lets you answer these once, clearly.

### 4. Not everyone uses Instagram

Some customers, such as parents, older people or business buyers, may prefer to look up a business on a website rather than create an account or scroll a feed. [ADD SOURCE: any current figures about how people research local businesses online]

### 5. Posts disappear in the feed

A reel or post gets attention for a short while, then it fades. A website page can be found by someone months from now.

### 6. It can look less established

For many customers, a business with a clean website, a clear address and a contact form feels more established than one that exists only as a social profile. This matters more for services where trust is important: clinics, schools, professional services and anything with higher prices.

## What a website adds

- **A permanent home** that you control
- **Search visibility,** so people who have never seen your Instagram can find you on Google
- **Clear information** about services, area, timings and how to contact you
- **Proof and trust,** with testimonials you have permission to share, photos and answers to common questions
- **A way to measure,** such as seeing how many people visit and how many contact you
- **A destination for ads,** since both Google Ads and Meta Ads often work better when they lead to a page built for the offer

## When you might not need a website yet

Honesty matters here. You may be fine without one for now if:

- You run a very small, home-based or hobby business with a handful of regular customers
- All your customers already find you through people they know
- You are still testing whether the business idea works

Even then, a Google Business Profile and a WhatsApp Business account are inexpensive ways to look professional and be found. A full website can wait until the business is steady.

## When you should build a website soon

You should seriously consider one if:

- You depend on new customers from your local area
- You offer services that people research before buying
- You want to run Google Ads or invest in SEO
- Your prices are high enough that trust matters
- You want to hire or work with partners who will look you up
- Instagram is your only channel, and a restriction on the account would hurt badly

## How to use Instagram and a website together

The best setup is simple:

1. **Instagram** attracts attention and shows personality.
2. **Google Business Profile** helps local customers find you.
3. **A website** explains clearly and builds trust.
4. **WhatsApp** makes it easy to talk.

Link them together. Put your website and WhatsApp link in your Instagram bio. Add your Instagram handle and feed highlights to your website. Use the same business name, phone number and photos everywhere.

## What a first website should include

You do not need a huge site. Start with:

- A home page that says what you do and where
- A page for each main service or product
- An about page with your story and a photo
- A contact page with your phone, WhatsApp, address or service area and a short form
- A few real photos from your work

Instagram is a great place to be seen. A website is a great place to be found and trusted. For most businesses that depend on local customers, having both works better than choosing one.
