If you run a small business in India, you have probably been told you should "do ads". Then comes the question that confuses almost everyone: Google Ads or Meta Ads (Instagram and Facebook)?

The honest answer is that they do different jobs. This guide explains the difference in simple language so you can choose the one that fits your business, or decide how to use both.

## The simple difference

- **Google Ads** reach people who are *searching*. They typed something like "AC repair near me" or "MBA coaching in Navi Mumbai", so they already want something.
- **Meta Ads** reach people who are *browsing*. They are scrolling through Instagram or Facebook and see your ad. They were not looking for you, but your offer might catch their interest.

Think of Google as a shop sign on the road where people are already looking for your type of shop. Think of Meta as a poster in a place where many people walk by.

## How Google Ads work

When someone searches on Google, ads can appear above or below the normal results. You choose the searches you want to appear for, write a short ad, set a daily budget and pay when someone clicks (the usual model for search ads).

**What Google Ads are good at**

- Catching people who need something now
- Services people look up in a hurry: repairs, clinics, locksmiths, lawyers, event venues
- Showing up while your SEO is still growing
- Tracking calls and form enquiries clearly

**What to watch out for**

- Clicks cost money even if the person does not contact you
- Popular searches can be expensive
- The wrong search terms waste budget, so negative keywords matter
- Ads stop when your budget stops

## How Meta Ads work

Meta Ads run on Instagram, Facebook and some other Meta apps. You choose who should see the ad by location, age and interests, add a photo or short video, and pick a goal: messages on WhatsApp, form leads, website visits or post engagement.

**What Meta Ads are good at**

- Showing offers, launches, events and admissions
- Visual businesses: food, fashion, beauty, fitness, interiors
- Reaching people before they know they need you
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads, which suit how many Indian customers prefer to talk

**What to watch out for**

- Many people tap or watch without any intention to buy
- Weak photos or unclear offers lead to poor results
- Leads can be low quality if the targeting or form is too loose
- You need to reply quickly, or the enquiry goes cold

## A quick way to choose

Ask yourself: **do people search for my business when they need it?**

| If your business is... | Start with... | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent or need-based (repairs, clinics, legal help) | Google Ads | People are searching right now |
| Visual or impulse-based (cafes, salons, fashion) | Meta Ads | Photos and offers catch attention |
| A new launch, event or admission drive | Meta Ads, then Google | Build awareness, then catch searches |
| Local shop or service | Google Business Profile first, then ads | Free visibility comes first |

This is a starting point, not a rule. Your own customers may behave differently, which is why small tests matter.

## Do not forget the free options

Ads cost money every time. Before spending, make sure the free basics are in place:

- A complete Google Business Profile ([step-by-step guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-business-profile-step-by-step/))
- A fast, mobile-friendly website with a clear contact button
- A WhatsApp Business profile with a greeting message and quick replies

Ads send more people to these. If they are weak, you pay for visitors who leave.

## How to start without wasting money

1. **Pick one goal.** For example: phone calls, WhatsApp messages or form enquiries.
2. **Pick one platform** to begin with.
3. **Set a small budget** that you can afford to spend while learning. [ADD SOURCE: platform budget guidance]
4. **Set up tracking** before you spend anything. Without it, you cannot tell what worked.
5. **Run for long enough** to learn. Changing everything every day makes results hard to read.
6. **Reply fast.** Enquiries from ads are warmest in the first few minutes.
7. **Review weekly.** Stop what does not work and keep what does.

## Common mistakes

- **Boosting posts** without a goal. The "Boost" button is easy but gives you little control.
- **Targeting too wide.** "Everyone in Maharashtra" is rarely right for a local business.
- **Sending people to the home page** instead of a page about the exact offer.
- **No tracking.** If you cannot count enquiries, you cannot improve.
- **Giving up too early,** or never giving it a proper try.
- **Mixing up ad spend with fees.** The money paid to Google or Meta is separate from any agency fee, and should be paid from your own account.

## So which one should you pick?

If customers search for what you offer, begin with Google Ads. If you sell something people discover by browsing, begin with Meta Ads. If you are not sure, run a small test of one, measure real enquiries and decide from there.
